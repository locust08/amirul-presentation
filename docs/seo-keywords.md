## How to apply a keyword

1. Open the owning entry in the admin → **SEO** panel.
2. Title: lead with the keyword, keep it under ~60 characters. Example: `Seafood Restaurant in Genting Highlands | DAFU Seafood`.
3. Description: one sentence that includes the keyword and a reason to visit, under ~155 characters.
4. Use the keyword naturally once in the tagline or story, and in at least one photo's alt text.
5. Do the same in the 中文 translation with the Chinese keyword.

Also set up **Google Business Profile** for each outlet with the same name, address and hours as the site — for "near me" and map searches this matters as much as the website.
